Our Story

A better cup, on University Ave.

Kaffeine is a downtown Toronto coffee shop built on a simple idea: serve the kind of cup you'd happily walk a few extra blocks for. We source thoughtfully, dial in every shot, and pour with the same care whether it's your morning ritual or your afternoon reset.

Step in off University Avenue and you'll find a warm, unhurried space — good espresso, honest brews, and a team that's glad you stopped by. No fuss, just coffee done right.
